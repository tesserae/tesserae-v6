# Greek function-word lemmas (2026-10-09)
#
# Source: CLTK's Greek stopword list (cltk.stops.grc.STOPS, cltk 1.5.0,
# 126 surface forms) with accents, apostrophes and breathings removed and
# final sigma written as sigma, because the literary lemma cache and the
# documents index store Greek lemmas in that unaccented form (for example
# ο, και, ειμι, αυτοσ, ουτοσ). Hand-checked against the top 70 lemmas by
# token frequency in a random 150-work sample of the literary cache: every
# function word among them (article, conjunction, particle, preposition,
# pronoun, ειμι) is covered; verbs and nouns such as λεγω, εχω, ποιεω,
# λογοσ, ανηρ are deliberately absent. Three pronoun lemmas missing from
# the list but frequent in the cache are added: εκεινοσ, ημεισ, υμεισ. Used by
# scripts/reuse/build_documents_reuse_table.py.

Greek headword table: source licence and attribution
=====================================================

data/lemma_tables/greek_headwords.json is built by
scripts/corpus/build_greek_headwords.py from the headwords (and only the
headwords) of the Perseus Digital Library's TEI text of

    Henry George Liddell, Robert Scott, Henry Stuart Jones,
    A Greek-English Lexicon (Oxford, 1940),
    machine readable text, Perseus Project, Tufts University.

The Perseus text is published under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 United States licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/us/). The table maps each
accent-stripped spelling to the accented LSJ headwords that reduce to it; it
contains no entry text. Built 2026-11-08 from 116,497 entries (63,230
spellings).

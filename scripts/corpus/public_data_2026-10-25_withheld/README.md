One-off scripts of 2026-10-25 that rebuilt three Latin public archives (la_index.db.tar.gz, window_texts.db.tar.gz, passage_index_la.tar.gz) without the works from Musisque Deoque, Corpus Thomisticum and augustinus.it and without the restricted work augustine.de_doctrina_christiana. Paths point at the staging folder ~/tesserae-backups/public_data_2026-10-25_withheld/ on the server. Run record: docs/DATA_OPERATIONS.md, 2026-10-25. make_id_list.py builds the id list from the credit rows (build_text_archives.withheld_reason) and the registry, filter_window_texts_withheld.py and filter_slice.py filter the window-text store and the passage slice, check_la_index.py checks the filtered index, make_pages.py, pack_all.py and verify_archives.py produce and verify the archives and page files.

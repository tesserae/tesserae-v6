# Persian

**Content index only.** Not yet a lexical search language in production.

- 218,213 passage windows, the largest single slice of the content index.
- Texts from the Chronological Persian Poetry Dataset, based on Ganjoor
  (ganjoor.net), freely available. Credit both.
- 19 authors dated as of 2026-08-25, Rudaki through Parvin, with dynastic era
  labels (Samanid, Ghaznavid, Seljuk, Ilkhanid, Timurid, Safavid, Mughal,
  Modern). **These labels are editorial and want a specialist.**
- A separate lexical index exists in `~/tesserae-persian` (`fa_index.db`) on
  branch `feature/persian-ship`, not merged.

**The limitation that matters, and it is stated to readers.** Persian poetic
intertextuality works substantially through FORM. A *javab* or *istiqbal*
answers a predecessor in the same metre, rhyme and radif, and can do so with
almost no shared vocabulary. Content descriptions capture content, not form, so
that whole mode of response is invisible to this index. Saying so is part of
using it honestly.

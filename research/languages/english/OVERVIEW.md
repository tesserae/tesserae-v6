# English

**Live.** ~162 works: the King James Bible, the World English Bible, Milton,
Spenser, Bunyan, the Romantics, and other pre-1930 literary texts.

- In the content index and in Theme Search.
- Public domain throughout, which is why the English slice of the released
  passage index carries no restriction.
- `sound` and `edit_distance` default to 0.0 in the `english` weight profile:
  useful when chosen deliberately, noisy otherwise.

**Active:** enlarging the corpus for an English-only-scholar demonstration.
Stage 1 in progress; prose giants (Holinshed, North) deferred.
See `project_english_corpus_expansion` in memory.
